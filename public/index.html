BEHIND THE PREDICTION

A lens into the models

Two tasks, one scenario. Explore classification of traffic impact and regression of geographic coordinates.

▥

Traffic impact

Four severity classes describe the effect on traffic. Class probabilities reflect the model output, not a guarantee.

⌖

Estimated location

Latitude and longitude are regression estimates. They do not identify a verified incident or an exact crash location.

Model limitations: Original preprocessing fixes twilight to Day and estimates wind chill as temperature minus 5°F. County options are not filtered by state. Coordinates are informational model estimates, not verified incident locations.
